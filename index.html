Private social operations workspace

One workspace. Four distinct brands.

Fourfold helps one authorized owner plan, review, schedule, publish and measure organic social content for AZ Pest Shield, Solartelligence, StreamFlicker and CarPartsRadar. Brand profiles and connected accounts are kept separate.

Why Fourfold requests Google access

When the owner connects a YouTube channel, Fourfold uses Google authorization to confirm the exact channel, read its channel and video information, upload content permitted by the owner’s publishing settings, read comments for owned conversations, and manage those comments or videos when the owner enables the relevant action. Fourfold stores the resulting authorization securely and can refresh access without asking the owner to sign in for each scheduled task.

The YouTube connection is optional. Public uploads remain subject to YouTube API eligibility and Fourfold’s publishing controls.

How it works

  • Connect an account through its official authorization page and map its identity to one brand.
  • Keep brand-specific media, drafts, approvals, schedules and results in one owner-controlled workspace.
  • Pause all automated work or individual accounts; review activity and disconnect accounts when needed.

This information site is public so people can understand the application. The operational workspace is restricted to its owner and is not offered as a public sign-up service.

Read the privacy policy for the specific Google data Fourfold uses, how it is stored, and how to remove it.
